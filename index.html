Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Minato collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Minato, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

To run the leveling system, Minato stores the following:

  • User data (per server): your Discord user ID and the server ID, along with your XP, level, message counts, voice activity time, weekly and monthly statistics, daily reward streak, and any XP restriction set by a server administrator.
  • Server settings: configuration chosen by server administrators, such as the command prefix, XP rates, XP and drop channels, level-up channel, level-up message and message type, role rewards, multipliers, disabled commands and restricted roles.
  • Images and links: if an administrator sets a custom rank card background or level-up image, the image is uploaded to a third-party image host (ImgBB) and only its link is saved in our database.

2. What We Do Not Store

Minato reads messages only to detect activity for XP and to respond to commands. We do not store the content of your messages, and we do not collect passwords, payment information, email addresses or other sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Information

  • To calculate and display XP, levels, rank cards, leaderboards and rewards.
  • To apply each server's configuration.
  • To keep the Bot working, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing of Information

Leaderboards and rank cards are visible to other members of the servers where you use Minato, and the global leaderboard may show your Discord username and avatar to other users. We only share data with the services needed to operate the Bot (for example, our database host and the image host), or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

Your data is kept for as long as the Bot is used in the relevant server, so that your progress is not lost. Server administrators can reset XP and levels at any time using the Bot's admin commands.

6. Your Choices and Data Removal

You can request access to or deletion of the data stored about you at any time by contacting us in the Support Server. Server administrators can also ask us to delete their server's configuration. Removing Minato from a server stops all further collection for that server.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data we store, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Minato is intended for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe this has happened, please contact us so we can remove the data.
